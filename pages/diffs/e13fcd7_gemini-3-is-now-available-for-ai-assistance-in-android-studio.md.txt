For developers using Gemini in Android Studio at no-cost (Default Model), we are rolling out limited access to Gemini 3 with a 1 million token size window. For higher usage rate limits and longer sessions with Agent Mode, [use a Gemini API key](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/add-api-key) to leverage Gemini 3 in Android Studio for the highest tier of AI capability.
