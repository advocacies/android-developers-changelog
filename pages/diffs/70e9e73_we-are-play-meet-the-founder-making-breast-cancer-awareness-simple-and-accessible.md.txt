 ### The app is always evolving. What is next for Know Your Lemons?
 
 We recently added a feature for companies to offer a breast health benefit to their employees, which helps them get on the right screening plan and connects them to resources like genetic counseling. The next big feature we're adding is an AI-powered matchmaking tool for newly diagnosed patients. It will connect them with patient advocacy organizations that are specific to their needs---whether it's a certain type of breast cancer or support for parents with young children. Most patients don't know these resources exist, so we bring the help directly to them.
