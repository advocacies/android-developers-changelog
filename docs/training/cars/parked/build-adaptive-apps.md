# Build adaptive apps Stay organized with collections Save and categorize content based on your preferences.





Build your app to run on form factors other than car displays, including phones,
tablets, foldables, ChromeOS devices, TVs, and XR.

Use Compose APIs that automatically provide different layouts and different
navigation controls for different screen sizes, shapes, and orientations.

Extend your app's usability. Optimize the user experience on every user device.
